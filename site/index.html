HELP & CLARIFICATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
about joining ILM Colleges.

What are the minimum eligibility criteria for admission? +

Students must have passed their Matriculation (SSC) or equivalent O-Levels. Minimum 60% marks are required for Science groups (Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering), while minimum 50% marks are required for ICS and I.Com.

Are need-based and kinships scholarships offered? +

Yes! In addition to top-tier merit scholarships (up to 100%), ILM Group of Colleges offers siblings (kinship) discounts, teacher's ward concessions, and orphan/financial aid allowances.

Can I transfer from another college to ILM? +

Transfers are facilitated subject to seat availability, board NOC, and verification of intermediate Part-I marks from recognized BISE boards.

What co-curricular and sports activities are available? +

Campuses host annual sports galas, science exhibitions, debate competitions, educational tours, and leadership youth clubs throughout the academic calendar.
